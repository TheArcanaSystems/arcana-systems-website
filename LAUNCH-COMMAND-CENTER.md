## Best-Fit Buyer

Prioritize founder-led service businesses where growth is being constrained by operational dependency.

Strong signals:
- The founder routes too many decisions and exceptions.
- Client handoffs lose information or require repeated follow-up.
- SOPs are incomplete, stale, or held in tribal knowledge.
- Teams copy information across multiple tools.
- Leadership cannot see reliable status without asking people.
- Onboarding, delivery, reporting, or billing depends on manual coordination.
- The business is functioning, but scale requires more structure.

## Positioning Rules

Lead with the operating problem, not a software platform.

Use:
- founder dependency
- operational friction
- fragmented handoffs
- unreliable visibility

Do not lead with:
